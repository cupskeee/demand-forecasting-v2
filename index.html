Demand Forecasting v2

A demand forecasting app that runs entirely on your own device. Add monthly sales as a CSV file, or open one of four built-in examples, and four models are fitted in your browser: XGBoost, SARIMAX, Elastic Net and Holt-Winters.

What you get

The app proposes the month column, the sales column and the external factors, such as price or promotion, then reports problems with the file in plain words. Each model is tested on months that were held back from it. The results put the best model first, with a chart of history, back-test and forecast, and a table comparing all four on MAE, RMSE, MAPE and R². A what-if lets you change the future values of the factors, for example price up 5 percent, and compare the result with the baseline.

What runs it

The forecasting is done by open-source projects: Pyodide, scikit-learn, statsmodels, XGBoost, pandas and NumPy. They are downloaded once, about 50 MB, and every file is checked against a published checksum. There is no server and no account, and nothing you import or produce is sent anywhere. After the first visit the app works with the network off.
